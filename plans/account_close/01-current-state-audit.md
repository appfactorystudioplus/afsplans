## 1.2 What creation does that deletion must reverse

From `MembershipController::store()` (lines 109–421) and the flows that run later. **Creation
touches these; the plan's purge reverses each** — cross-referenced to the erasers in
[05 §5.3](05-purge-engine.md):

| Created by | What | Reversed by |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| `store()` step 2 | `laravel_direct_payment_payers`, `_payer_customers` (via `PayerService`) | Eraser `billing` |
| step 3 | Claim of `laravel_direct_payment_subscriptions` (sets `payer_reference_num`; **no provider call is made at signup**, `membership_id` is the provider subscription id) | Eraser `billing` — **cancel at the provider** |
| step 4 | Checkpoint `ext_member_users` row (`/checkpoint/auth/register`), a session, and the Parse `MemberRegistry`/`MemberResource` rows (`/checkpoint/member/init`) | Erasers `checkpoint`, `parse` |
| step 5 | `brain_memberships` row | Last step of the purge |
| step 7 | `/mnt/node1/{ref}` via `--create-afs-member-storage` | Eraser `storage` |
| later flows | `brain_http_server_tenants`, `brain_dns_zones`, `brain_dns_subdomains`, `members_websites`, `members_website_deployment_progress`, mailboxes (`users_email` + Maildir + `vmailbox`) | Erasers `tenants`, `dns`, `mail` |

`adminStore()` (line 517) is a subset — no payer, no storage command, no welcome mail — so an
admin-created member has *fewer* things to reverse, and the purge must treat every step as
"nothing to do" rather than "error".
